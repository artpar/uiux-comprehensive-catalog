## Personalization Notes

- For newsletters, keep the email short and lead with why their readers would save it.
- For publications, do not ask for a bare backlink. Pitch a useful article idea and include the checklist as the companion resource.
- For directories, submit the checklist page directly with "UX resource", "design systems", "UI patterns", "frontend implementation", and "AI-generated UI" tags where available.
- Avoid mass-sending the same message. Reference one recent issue, article, or resource category for each prospect.
